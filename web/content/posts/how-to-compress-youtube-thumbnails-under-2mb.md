Exporting a 1280x720 or 1920x1080 thumbnail as an uncompressed PNG frequently produces a 3MB to 5MB file—triggering YouTube Studio's 2MB upload limit error.

## 1. Export at 1280x720 (16:9 Aspect Ratio)
YouTube recommends 1280x720 pixels with a minimum width of 640 pixels. Exporting at 4K resolution adds unnecessary file weight without improving mobile feed clarity.

## 2. Convert Heavy PNGs to Optimized JPEG
Unless your thumbnail relies on transparent layers, high-quality JPEG compression at 80–85% quality preserves crisp facial details and bold text while cutting file size by up to 75%.
