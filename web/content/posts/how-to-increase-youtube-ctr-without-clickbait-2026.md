If your video has strong watch time but low views, your packaging—the combination of thumbnail and title—is holding back your impressions.

## 1. Create a Curiosity Gap Between Title and Thumbnail
Never repeat the exact same words on your thumbnail and in your title. Let the thumbnail show the visual result or tension while the title explains the specific payoff.

## 2. Simplify Visual Focal Points to Three Elements
Crowded thumbnails fail on mobile feeds. Limit every frame to one clear subject, one visual contrast cue, and three bold words maximum.

## 3. Audit Dark Mode vs. Light Mode Contrast
Test how your thumbnail edges separate from both dark and white backgrounds using our [Thumbnail & Title Mobile Previewer](/tools/thumbnail-previewer) before uploading to YouTube Studio.
