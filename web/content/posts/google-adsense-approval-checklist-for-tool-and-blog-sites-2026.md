Websites that combine interactive browser tools with original, well-structured tutorials perform strongly in both Google Search and AdSense reviews—provided core trust signals are in place.

## 1. Essential Legal & Trust Pages
Make sure your footer clearly links to an **About Us**, **Contact**, and **Privacy Policy & Disclaimer** page so reviewers and users can verify site ownership and data practices.

## 2. Original Content Depth & Clean Navigation
Publish at least 15 original, helpful articles (600+ words each) organized into clear categories, with zero broken links or placeholder pages.

## 3. Fast Mobile Core Web Vitals
Lightweight, browser-based utilities that run without heavy third-party scripts keep page load speeds fast and bounce rates low across mobile devices.
