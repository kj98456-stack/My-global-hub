Earnings for 1 lakh (100,000) views in India depend heavily on video length, audience geography, and advertiser demand in your specific category.

## 1. Long-Form AdSense Payouts for 1 Lakh Views
* **Entertainment, Vlogs & Gaming:** Typically ₹4,000 to ₹10,000 per 100,000 views.
* **Tech Tutorials, AI & Creator Tools:** Typically ₹12,000 to ₹28,000 per 100,000 views.
* **Personal Finance & Software Reviews:** Often ₹25,000 to ₹55,000+ per 100,000 views.
