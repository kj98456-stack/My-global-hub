Over 70% of YouTube watch time happens on smartphones, yet most creators design their thumbnails zoomed in at 100% on a large monitor. When that same image shrinks down to a mobile home feed card, thin fonts disappear and long titles get cut off mid-sentence.

## 1. Check the 3-Word Mobile Readability Rule
If a viewer scrolling fast on a 6-inch screen cannot read your thumbnail text in under one second, they will scroll past. Keep thumbnail text to 3–4 bold words with strong contrast against the background.

## 2. Keep Video Titles Under 60 Characters
YouTube allows up to 100 characters in a title, but mobile feeds typically truncate titles around the 55–60 character mark. Put your strongest hook and primary keyword in the first 50 characters.

## 3. Test Both Dark Mode and Light Mode Feeds
Half your audience browses YouTube in Dark Mode and half in Light Mode. A dark thumbnail border can blend into a dark feed, while a bright white background can wash out in light mode. Use our free **Thumbnail & Title Mobile Previewer** right on the homepage to test both themes before publishing.
