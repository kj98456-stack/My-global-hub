YouTube officially notes that tags play a minimal role compared to your title, thumbnail, and description—yet using the 500-character box strategically still helps capture common misspellings and topic variations.

## 1. Put Your Exact Primary Keyword First
Your first two tags should match your core video title and primary search query verbatim. Follow them with 4–6 natural variations viewers actually type into search.

## 2. Include Common Spelling Variations
If your topic involves technical terms, brand names, or Hinglish phrasing, include the most common spelling variations so search queries connect accurately.
