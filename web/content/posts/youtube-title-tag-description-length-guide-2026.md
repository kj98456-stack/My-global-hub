Optimizing your video metadata takes less than two minutes once you know the exact character thresholds YouTube uses on mobile and desktop search results.

## 1. Video Title Limit: 100 Characters (Aim for 55–60)
While the hard limit is 100 characters, keeping your title between 55 and 60 characters prevents awkward truncation on mobile home feeds and suggested video sidebars.

## 2. Description Hook: First 125 Characters Matter Most
YouTube gives you 5,000 characters for your description, but viewers only see the first 120–125 characters before tapping "More." Place your primary search phrase and core value proposition in those opening two lines.

## 3. Tags Limit: 500 Characters Total
YouTube caps the entire tag field—including commas—at 500 characters. Focus on 8–12 specific multi-word phrases rather than dozens of single words, and verify your exact character count with our free **YouTube Title, Description & Tag Analyzer** before saving.
