A structured description helps YouTube's search system understand your video topic while guiding viewers toward your tools, playlists, and affiliate recommendations.

## 1. Lines 1–2: The 125-Character Search Hook
Write two natural sentences summarizing the exact problem your video solves, including your primary search phrase within the first 125 characters.

## 2. Lines 3–6: Primary Call-to-Action & Resource Links
Place your most important link—whether a free tool, guide, or recommended product—right below the fold so interested viewers find it immediately.

## 3. Timestamps & Related Videos
Add clean `00:00` chapter markers so Google Search can index key moments directly in search snippets, and validate your character counts with our [YouTube Title, Description & Tag Analyzer](/tools/youtube-seo-analyzer).
