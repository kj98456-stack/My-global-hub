Viewers will forgive average 1080p phone video, but echoey audio or harsh overhead room lighting causes immediate drop-offs in the first 10 seconds.

## 1. Wired vs. Wireless Collar Microphones Under ₹2,000
For desk tutorials and talking-head videos, a reliable omnidirectional lavalier mic or entry-level USB condenser microphone eliminates room echo cleanly. If you shoot outdoors or move around, dual-channel wireless clip-on mics under ₹2,500 keep voice levels consistent.

## 2. Soft Key Lighting Over Expensive Cameras
Place a single 10-inch or 12-inch bi-color LED ring light or compact softbox panel at a 45-degree angle to your face. Clean front lighting makes any modern smartphone sensor look sharp and reduces grain in dark backgrounds.
