Short-form views scale rapidly in India, but RPM (Revenue Per Mille) varies sharply depending on audience location, watch retention, and content niche.

## 1. Typical Indian YouTube Shorts RPM
For predominantly Indian traffic, Shorts RPM typically ranges between ₹4 and ₹15 per 1,000 engaged views ($0.05 to $0.18 USD). Channels that attract viewers from the US, UK, or UAE can see Shorts RPM climb above $0.25–$0.40 USD.

## 2. Long-Form vs. Shorts Revenue Math
A single long-form tutorial in tech, finance, or creator education often earns ₹80 to ₹250 per 1,000 views—nearly 15x higher than Shorts. Use Shorts as a top-of-funnel discovery engine to funnel subscribers into your higher-RPM long-form videos.
