Uploading the wrong aspect ratio or oversized image file leads to black sidebars, blurry previews, or rejected uploads in YouTube Studio.

## 1. Recommended Dimensions & Aspect Ratio
* **Resolution:** 1280 x 720 pixels (minimum width 640 pixels).
* **Aspect Ratio:** Strictly 16:9 widescreen.
* **Maximum File Size:** 2MB for standard videos.

## 2. Keep Text Out of the Bottom-Right Corner
YouTube places the video duration timestamp (`12:45`) directly over the bottom-right corner of every thumbnail. Never put key words or facial expressions in that zone.
